# Minecraft 1.20.4 Progression Knowledge (bot-readable, injected per task type)

Each `##` section is injected alone into escalation prompts. Keep sections
short: every line costs free-tier quota on every escalation.

## GEAR
Gear ladder: wood < stone < iron < diamond < netherite. Leather armor needs
only cows and no furnace — craft it first. Iron needs stone pick + furnace +
fuel. Diamond needs IRON pick and deep mining. Shield = 6 planks + 1 iron.
Bow = 3 stick + 3 string (spiders drop string). Netherite = diamond gear +
smithing template + netherite ingot at a smithing table (late game).

## ORES
Best Y levels (1.20): coal anywhere, iron surface/highlands, copper mid,
gold badlands, lapis/redstone deep, DIAMOND Y -59. Branch-mine at diamond
level: 2-high tunnels, spaced 2 apart, torch every 6. Carry a water bucket
against lava. NEVER dig straight down. Stone pick mines iron/copper/coal.
Only IRON+ pick drops diamond/gold/redstone — wrong tier = block breaks
with NO drop.

## ENCHANT
Enchant table = 2 diamond + 4 obsidian + 1 book (sugar cane + leather).
Obsidian needs DIAMOND pick (water on lava). Lapis spent per enchant.
Anvil repairs and combines gear. Enchanted diamond beats plain netherite
plans — enchanting comes before any nether ambitions.

## STRUCTURES
Loot priority near spawn: village blacksmith chest (iron, sometimes diamond)
> desert temple (dig in from the SIDE — floor hides TNT) > mineshaft
(rails, chests, but cave spiders) > shipwreck (buried treasure map) >
igloo basement (trapped villagers). Far/dangerous: jungle temple
(tripwires), pillager outpost (crossbows), woodland mansion (evokers drop
totems), ocean monument (needs water breathing). Stronghold: throw Eye of
Ender (blaze powder + ender pearl), follow flight, carry spares — eyes
break. Trial chambers (1.20): trial spawners, breeze mobs, mace parts.

## VILLAGERS
Villages spawn in plains/desert/savanna/taiga/snow. Loot blacksmith first,
then set up: job blocks assign professions (lectern=librarian, composter=
farmer, blast furnace=armorer, smithing table=toolsmith, fletching table=
fletcher). Farmers buy crops, librarians buy paper and sell ENCHANTED
BOOKS. Cure a zombie villager (weakness + golden apple) for PERMANENT
discounts. Winning a raid gives Hero of the Village (temporary discounts).
Protect villagers: they die, prices die with them.

## HUSBANDRY
Breed cows/sheep with wheat, pigs with carrot/potato/beetroot, chickens
with seeds. Cook everything: beef/pork/chicken/mutton/cod/salmon/rabbit,
plus potato->baked (farm staple) and kelp->dried (fast snack). Never eat
raw chicken (hunger risk). Wheat farm + composter + farmer villager =
emerald pipeline.

## COMBAT
Armor tiers: leather < gold < chain < iron < diamond < netherite. Full iron
before night roaming; leather beats naked. Shield blocks arrows and melee
— raise it vs skeletons. Strafe skeleton arrows, never look at endermen,
kill creepers at range or with bow, pillagers mean nearby outpost. Sleep
through the night whenever a bed exists — no shame, no loot is worth it
before iron.

## BUILDING
Night 1: dirt/plank hole, torch inside. Then: walls + door + torches every
6 blocks (mobs spawn at light 0). Then: farm plot near water, fenced
animal pen, chest room, furnace row. Then: shared house with beds (sleep
together = skip night), trading corner with job blocks, wall + gate.
Build near village when found — free walls, free beds, free golem guard.

## PLAN
Full arc: stone tools -> furnace -> leather/iron armor -> bed -> iron pick
-> DIAMOND at Y -59 -> obsidian -> enchant table -> village trade hall ->
structure loot -> raid/totems -> nether fortress (blazes) -> stronghold ->
dragon. Nether/End travel has NO bot support yet — plan overworld stages
only, and note nether/end as future. Civilization wins come from trade
halls, shared farms, and surviving nights together, not solo heroics.
